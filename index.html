A little context about the person, the curiosity, and the thinking behind the work.

My perspective

“I want to understand what happens beneath the surface.”

I'm Marang Babu — an undergraduate Physics student with a strong curiosity about technology and the systems that make it work.

I enjoy going beyond the surface: exploring Android internals, experimenting with custom kernels, working in command-line environments, and learning how software communicates with hardware.

My approach is hands-on. I learn by reading, testing, troubleshooting, documenting, and trying again. Not every experiment works the first time — and that is part of the process.

This website is my evolving record: a place for projects, technical notes, personal reflections, and the things I discover along the way.
