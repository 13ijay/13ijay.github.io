01 / A little about me

Not a finished story.
A meaningful beginning.

A perspective I carry

I have never believed that you need to have everything figured out before you begin.

My academic journey in physics encourages me to think carefully, notice patterns, and stay patient with difficult questions. Beyond my studies, I enjoy exploring ideas, taking on personal projects, and finding creative ways to turn curiosity into something real.

I'm learning to value the process as much as the outcome: the attempts that teach me something, the small improvements that add up, and the courage to start again with a little more understanding than before.

I'm still discovering where my path will lead. For now, my focus is simple — stay curious, do honest work, and keep becoming someone I'm proud of.

02 / What guides me

The things I choose
to stand for.

Not claims of perfection — just principles I want to keep practising, in my work and in the way I move through life.

01

Curiosity

Look beyond the obvious. Ask why, stay open to different perspectives, and never let being a beginner become a reason to stop exploring.

02

Consistency

Respect the quiet work that happens before results are visible. Small, repeated efforts can build skills and confidence over time.

03

Independence

Learn to think things through, take responsibility for decisions, and become capable of finding a way forward when the answer isn't obvious.

04

Creativity

Give ideas a personal touch. Care about the details, make room for imagination, and aim to create things that feel thoughtful and useful.
